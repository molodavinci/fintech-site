Our mission

Making global finance
truly accessible

Over 1.4 billion people worldwide remain excluded from basic financial services. Traditional banking is riddled with unnecessary friction, hidden fees, and geography-based discrimination.

VioletBank exists to eliminate those barriers. We believe that a student in Lagos, a freelancer in Manila, and a startup founder in Berlin should all have access to the same world-class financial infrastructure — instantly, affordably, and without compromise.

What drives us

Our values

These aren't wall posters. They're the principles every decision at VioletBank is measured against.

Security first

Every feature, every line of code, every policy starts with the question: is this safe for our users? Security is never an afterthought.

Radical transparency

No hidden fees, no confusing terms. We publish our fee schedules, exchange rates, and policy changes in plain English before they happen.

Speed as a feature

Waiting is a form of disrespect. We obsess over milliseconds — in our product and in our support response times. Fast is never fast enough.

Global inclusion

We design for the world — not just wealthy countries. Geography shouldn't determine whether someone has access to great financial services.

Relentless innovation

Finance is one of the oldest industries — and one of the least changed. We don't accept "that's how it's always been" as a reason to stay still.

User obsession

Our users are not a metric. They're the reason we exist. Every product decision is grounded in real feedback, real use cases, and real impact.

Our journey

Five years of
building the future

2020

Founded in Lagos & London

David and Priya meet at a fintech conference and discover a shared obsession with fixing cross-border payments. VioletBank is born in a co-working space in Lagos with $800K in pre-seed funding.

2021

Private beta launches

2,000 hand-picked early adopters test the first version. Feedback is intense and honest. The team rebuilds the transfer engine from scratch to achieve <10 second settlements — a milestone no competitor had hit.

2022

100K users & Series A

Public launch drives 100,000 sign-ups in the first 90 days. A $24M Series A from Sequoia and Ribbit Capital funds expansion into 40 new countries and the first version of the Business API.

2023

Enterprise tier & 140 countries

VioletBank Business launches with full API access, multi-user controls, and accounting integrations. Major enterprises including global NGOs and remote-first tech companies make the switch. Coverage expands to 140 countries.

2024

$1B processed milestone

VioletBank crosses $1 billion in total payment volume. The AI analytics engine ships, turning raw transaction data into actionable financial insights for every user — free and paid alike.

2025

500K members & $2.4B processed

Today, VioletBank serves over 500,000 individuals and 12,000 businesses across 140 countries, with $2.4B processed annually. We're just getting started — the world deserves better finance.
