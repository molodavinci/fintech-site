FAQ

Common questions

  • Is VioletBank FDIC insured?
    Yes. Deposits are held at our FDIC-member partner banks and are insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per institution, in line with standard FDIC coverage limits.
  • How long do international transfers take?
    Most transfers settle in under 10 seconds via our real-time rail network. In rare cases (certain corridors or weekend processing), transfers may take up to 1 business day. You'll always see an exact ETA before confirming.
  • What currencies does VioletBank support?
    We support 140+ currencies for both holding and conversion. You can see a full list in your account dashboard. Currency exchange uses real mid-market rates with a small transparent fee shown upfront.
  • How do I get API access for my business?
    API access is available on our Business plan. Once approved, you'll receive sandbox credentials instantly and can apply for production access after a brief technical review. Our average integration time is under 2 business days.
  • Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
    Absolutely. You can downgrade or cancel at any time from your account settings — no fees, no penalties. Your funds are always yours and can be withdrawn immediately upon closing.
  • What security measures protect my account?
    We use 256-bit AES encryption for all data, biometric and hardware-key 2FA, real-time transaction monitoring, and automated anomaly detection. We're SOC 2 Type II certified and conduct quarterly third-party penetration tests.
